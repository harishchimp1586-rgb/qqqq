# Rejoice Enterprise

A modern, professional, and mobile-responsive business website for **REJOICE ENTERPRISE**, specializing in quality eco-friendly disposable food-service products.

## 🌿 Business Overview
- **Business Name**: REJOICE ENTERPRISE
- **Contact Number**: 63744 50321
- **Focus Products**: Eco-friendly plates, cups, spoons, and disposable serving products for homes, shops, functions, events, and businesses.

## ✨ Features
- **Design**: Clean, premium, eco-friendly aesthetic featuring dark green, warm ivory, and subtle botanical elements.
- **Sticky Navigation**: Smooth fixed header with brand logo, nav links, and direct call CTA.
- **Hero Showcase**: Eyebrow badge, primary heading, call-to-action buttons (Call Now & WhatsApp Us), and store display showcase.
- **Product Catalog**: Dedicated interactive cards for:
  - Eco-Friendly Plates
  - Eco-Friendly Cups
  - Eco-Friendly Spoons
  - Disposable Serving Products
- **About Section**: Detailed business highlights and core quality points.
- **Why Choose Us**: 4 key feature highlights (🌱 Eco-Friendly, ⭐ Quality Products, 📦 Variety, 🤝 Friendly Service).
- **Contact Banner**: Dark green contact banner with direct telephone and WhatsApp quick-inquiry links.
- **Mobile Optimization**: Responsive layout with fixed bottom quick-action bar for one-tap calling and messaging.
